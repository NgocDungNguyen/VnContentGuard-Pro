🛡️ VnContentGuard Pro — Trợ lý AI giúp bạn an toàn hơn trên mạng xã hội Việt Nam.

Tin giả tràn lan. Bình luận độc hại khắp nơi. Lừa đảo ngày càng tinh vi. Bạn cần một công cụ đáng tin cậy để bảo vệ mình — và VnContentGuard Pro được sinh ra cho điều đó.

Được xây dựng trên nền tảng Google Gemini AI, VnContentGuard Pro phân tích nội dung trực tiếp trên trang web bạn đang đọc, cho bạn câu trả lời rõ ràng chỉ trong vài giây.

### Full Description

🛡️ VnContentGuard Pro — Your AI-powered safety companion for Vietnamese cyberspace.

Fake news. Toxic comments. Online scams. The Vietnamese internet can be overwhelming — but it doesn't have to be. VnContentGuard Pro uses Google Gemini AI to analyze content on any webpage in real-time, giving you clear, instant insights.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ WHAT IT DOES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📰 FACT-CHECK ARTICLES
Instantly assess credibility, spot misinformation and clickbait using multi-source verification. Every article gets a detailed summary (12–15 sentences), a credibility score from 0–100, and a clear verdict: Verified True, Likely True, Unclear, Likely False, or False. Know what's real before you share.

💬 SCAN TOXIC COMMENTS (4-LAYER DETECTION)
The most powerful toxic content detection for Vietnamese — combining 4 detection layers:
  • 🔍 Smart Regex — 50+ instant-match patterns
  • 🤖 Google Gemini AI — Understands Vietnamese context and nuance
  • 📊 Google Perspective API — Industry-standard toxicity model
  • 🧪 Detoxify — Deep learning multi-category classification

Comments are color-coded by severity: 🟢 Safe · 🟠 Warning · 🔴 Dangerous. Detects hate speech, violence, harassment, regional discrimination, scams, and gambling spam — all in Vietnamese.

⚡ SMART FILTERING — SAVES UP TO 80% OF API CALLS
Not every comment needs AI. The pre-processing pipeline quickly classifies obvious comments (clearly clean or clearly toxic), only sending ambiguous ones to AI. API savings are shown in the results.

🎭 SENTIMENT ANALYSIS
Understand the emotional tone of any article at a glance: Positive, Negative, or Neutral — with detailed confidence scores and intensity levels. Uses combined PhoBERT (Vietnamese-specialized AI) and keyword analysis.

📊 OVERALL RISK SCORE (0–100)
All analyses combined into one easy-to-understand score:
  • Fake news + Toxicity + Sentiment + Source reliability + Manipulation
  • Detailed breakdown for each component
  • Specific warnings and actionable recommendations

🚀 KEY FEATURES IN V4.9
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ REAL-TIME STREAMING RESULTS
No waiting for everything to finish. Results stream module by module (summary → sentiment → toxicity → fact-check → comments → risk) with a visual progress bar. You can close the popup — the scan runs in the background and results appear when you reopen.

⚡ INSTANT MODE (OFFLINE)
Preliminary results appear INSTANTLY via local regex analysis while waiting for full AI analysis. Detect dangerous content immediately without any server connection.

📜 SCAN HISTORY
Stores your last 20 scans with article titles, domains, risk scores, and timestamps. Click any entry to revisit the page.

📊 COMPARE 2 PAGES
Select 2 scans from history for a side-by-side comparison: risk, sentiment, toxicity, credibility, comments — with a conclusion on which page is more trustworthy.

📥 EXPORT PDF REPORTS
Export complete analysis results as a beautifully formatted HTML report with PDF print support. Includes summary, risk score, toxic comment table, sentiment, fact-check, and recommendations.

👍👎 FEEDBACK & LEARNING
Rate the analysis results — agree or disagree. Your feedback helps the AI improve accuracy for that domain over time. You can enter specific corrections when the AI gets it wrong.

🚩 COMMUNITY REPORTING & BLOCKLIST
Report dangerous websites (misinformation, scams, hate speech, spam...) to the VnContentGuard community. When enough reports accumulate, the site is automatically flagged with a warning for all users.

🔒 PARENTAL CONTROL
Protect children and family members:
  • Toggle on/off with a PIN code (4–6 digits)
  • Customizable risk threshold (30–100)
  • Automatically blocks pages exceeding the threshold with a styled block page
  • Enter PIN to temporarily unlock

⚠️ AUTOMATIC CONTENT WARNINGS
Community-blocklisted websites are automatically redirected to a warning page — showing report count, risk level, and letting you go back safely or proceed at your own risk. Option to "Always Allow" for trusted sites.

📈 WEEKLY SAFETY REPORT
Automatically compiles your scanning activity from the past 7 days:
  • Total pages scanned, risk distribution (safe / medium / high)
  • Top 5 riskiest and most-scanned domains
  • Feedback statistics and accuracy rate
  • PDF export support

🔔 RISK NOTIFICATIONS
Automatic browser notifications when medium-or-higher risk content is detected (≥50/100). Click the notification to open the flagged page.

⚡ AUTO-SCAN
Toggle it on — VnContentGuard automatically scans when you visit Facebook, VnExpress, Tuổi Trẻ, Dân Trí, and other supported sites. Smart rate-limiting: each URL scanned only once per 30 minutes.

🌙 DARK MODE
Complete dark theme — easy on the eyes for nighttime browsing.

📊 API USAGE DASHBOARD
Monitor system status right in the popup: daily usage / limit, available API keys, cache hit rate, and server uptime.
